# Design It Twice

This document encourages designing multiple solutions before committing to one.

## The Principle

Before implementing any significant feature, design it at least twice with different approaches. Compare the trade-offs before choosing.

## Design Review Checklist

- [ ] Have we explored at least 2 different approaches?
- [ ] Have we documented the trade-offs of each?
- [ ] Have we considered maintainability?
- [ ] Have we considered scalability?
- [ ] Have we considered development time?
